From exception to rolling again

Built for dispatchers who live in exceptions. Three moves, every wave.

  1. Scan the board

    Quiet metrics for open, blocked, and en route — then the table does the real work. No rainbow KPI collage.

  2. Filter the stuck few

    Status and yard stay filterable (and shareable via GET in production) so a lead can jump into your view mid-shift.

  3. Reassign, then move on

    Bulk-select blocked runs. Confirm cancels carefully. Tools used all night should not animate at you.
