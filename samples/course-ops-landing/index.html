The weekly rhythm

From roll call to the right conversation

Built for people who teach in public and grade in private. Three moves, every week.

  1. Scan the roster

    Quiet metrics show active, at-risk, and completed — then the table does the real work. No rainbow card collage.

  2. Filter to the stuck few

    Cohort, status, and name search stay in the URL so you can share a view with a co-instructor before office hours.

  3. Nudge, then move on

    Bulk-select who needs a poke. Confirm removes carefully. Tools used daily should not animate at you.

Why it feels different

Dense when it helps. Quiet when it doesn’t.

Course ops is an operator tool, not a marketing site wearing a dashboard costume.
